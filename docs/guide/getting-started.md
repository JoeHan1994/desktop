# Getting Started

This guide walks you through your first login and the initial orientation of the Terraforge interface.

---

## Prerequisites

- A corporate PatchMyPC account
- A modern browser (Chrome 120+, Edge 120+, Firefox 121+, Safari 17+)

---

## Step 1 — Log In

1. Navigate to the [Terraforge URL](https://terraforge.southeastasia.cloudapp.azure.com/) in your browser.
2. You will be redirected to the **Login** page automatically.
3. Click **Sign in with PatchMyPC account**.

![Login page](/screenshots/login.png)

4. Complete the Microsoft authentication flow (credentials / MFA as required by your organisation).
5. On first login, you may see a permissions consent screen — click **Accept**.
6. You will be redirected back to Terraforge and land on the **Dashboard**.

::: tip Session duration
Your session stays active for **7 days**. After that, you'll be prompted to log in again.
:::

---

## Step 2 — Explore the Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of:

- **Session Quota** — your current session usage vs. your allocated limit
- **Shared With Me** — sessions shared by teammates
- **Log Monitoring** — active issues detected in your sessions
- **Active Work Items** — work items assigned to you, with priority highlights
- **Checkout Available** — pre-configured sessions ready for quick checkout

Below the KPI cards you'll find detail cards for **My Sessions**, **Shared With Me**, **Test Runs**, and **Work Items** with status breakdowns, charts, and direct links to each section.

All data auto-refreshes every 60 seconds–3 minutes depending on the data type.

Use the **left sidebar** to navigate between sections.

---

## Step 3 — Navigate the Sidebar

| Section        | Description                        |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard**  | Overview stats and quick actions   |
| **My Sessions** | Manage your test environments     |
| **Test Runs**  | Browse and trigger test executions |
| **Monitoring** | System health metrics              |
| **Work Items** | Linked work tracking items         |
